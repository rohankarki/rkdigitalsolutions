Project Overview

This demo shows how a restaurant or cafe can present food items, opening hours, contact details and online booking in a clean way.

  • Menu section
  • Booking call-to-action
  • Location and contact details
  • Mobile responsive layout

Technologies Used

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive design, clean layout and user-friendly structure.

Why This Helps Clients

A professional restaurant website helps customers view the menu, contact the business quickly and trust the brand before visiting.

RK Digital Solutions Website with Demo Portfolio

This version includes:
- Main business website
- Demo portfolio section
- 3 separate demo project pages
- Logo sample
- Flyer sample
- Video editing sample
- Pricing section
- Contact form placeholder

How to use:
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in your browser.
3. Replace your-email@example.com and phone number.
4. Replace Formspree YOUR_FORM_ID with your real Formspree form ID.
5. Upload the full folder to GitHub.
6. Connect the GitHub repo to Netlify.

Important:
The portfolio items are demo/sample projects. This is okay for starting. You can write:
"Demo projects created to show our design style and service quality."
